🍎 AgriConnect: Direct Farm-to-Buyer Mobile Platform
A user-friendly mobile application designed to eliminate middlemen in agricultural trade, allowing farmers to directly connect with consumers and businesses to negotiate fair, transparent prices for their produce.

✨ Key Features
For Farmers (Producers) 🧑‍🌾
Inventory Management: Easily list products, update quantities, set price per unit, and manage stock availability.

Product Showcase: Upload high-quality photos and detailed descriptions of produce.

Direct Sales Channel: Receive and manage direct purchase inquiries and orders from buyers.

For Buyers (Consumers/Businesses) 🛒
Seamless Browsing: Explore fresh produce listings with filters for category.

Shopping Cart & Checkout: Standard e-commerce functionality for adding items and placing orders.

Direct Negotiation: Communicate directly with farmers to negotiate bulk pricing or delivery terms.

Order Tracking: Monitor the status of current and past orders.

The application features separate flows for Buyers and Farmers, managed by user role authentication, along with multi-language support.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
This application is built with a focus on a modern, maintainable, and scalable architecture.

Language: Kotlin

Backend: Firebase (Authentication, Database, Storage).
